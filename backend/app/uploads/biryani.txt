Marination: Coat chicken pieces in yogurt, ginger-garlic paste, lemon juice, and spices like turmeric and red chili powder; let sit for at least 30 minutes. 
Rice Preparation: Soak basmati rice for 30 minutes, then parboil it in heavily salted, spice-infused water until 70-80% cooked.
Layering: In a heavy-bottomed pot, layer the marinated chicken at the bottom, followed by the parboiled rice, fried onions, fresh herbs (mint/coriander), and saffron-infused milk. 
Dum Cooking: Seal the pot with a damp muslin cloth and lid, then cook on low heat for 20–30 minutes to allow the rice to steam and absorb the chicken’s flavors.
The best biryanis are found in the streets of Main Pune City where Parsis are selling huge quantities maintainig their own taste and uniqueness
